Services

Three practices
One craft

01

Interpretation

I provide consecutive and liaison interpretation services from French to English and English to French. Depending on the situation, I can also support interpretation from Hindi to French. Interpretation is not only about word substitution. It is about listening under pressure, holding the message in memory, and delivering it in a way that sounds natural, respectful, and accurate in the target language. That makes interpretation services especially important for meetings, visits, delegations, institutional conversations, and public-facing events where misunderstanding can become expensive or embarrassing very quickly.

For live settings, I work to preserve meaning, register, and intent while keeping the conversation moving. In a business or delegation setting, the goal is smooth communication rather than visible language mechanics. That is why clients who need interpretation services usually value reliability, discretion, and calm delivery as much as linguistic skill.

02

Translation

My translation services cover academic documents, birth certificates, news articles, railway-related material, and other technical or institutional documents. As a French to English translator and English to French translator, I focus on accuracy, readability, natural, context-sensitive and suitable for the intended audience.

Different texts need different strategies. Academic and technical texts demand consistency and precision. Public communication needs tone and audience awareness. Administrative or document translation services often require formal wording and exact terminology. I approach each category accordingly, so the final result is not just correct in a narrow sense, but usable in the situation it was written for.

03

Proof Reading & Editing

Proofreading and editing are essential when the draft is already there but the finish is not. I review word choice, syntax, punctuation, and grammar in both English and French. If a document needs to read like it was written by a careful native-level user, I refine it line by line. This is especially useful for applications, letters, statements, reports, articles, and public-facing material where quality and credibility matter.

I can also help refine translated content that has been produced elsewhere. Sometimes a text is technically translated but still feels awkward, stiff, or inconsistent. In those cases, proofreading and editing can make the difference between a passable draft and a professional deliverable.

About me

Living between Languages.

I am a French translator, interpreter and proof reader based in New Delhi, India. My work is rooted in long conversation between Europe and the Indian subcontinent — a conversation carried, quietly, by language.

I hold a DALF C1 and work across education, public services and professional communication. I am a member of the Interpreters Association of India.

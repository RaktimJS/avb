About me

A life in three tongues.

I am a French educator, translator and interpreter based in New Delhi. My work is rooted in long conversation between Europe and the Indian subcontinent — a conversation carried, quietly, by language.

I hold a DALF C1 and work across education, public services and professional communication. I am a member of the Interpreters Association of India.
